# Velvet

> Velvet is a multimodal AI model R&D company (the "multimodal data lab") based in San Francisco, part of Y Combinator's Fall 2025 batch. It partners with frontier AI labs and enterprises to build state-of-the-art video datasets and evaluation frameworks that improve models' spatial reasoning and interactive, low-latency capabilities. Founded by Lucas Mantovani (CEO, ex Meta FAIR video data) and Lucas Tucker (CTO, ex Adobe).

Velvet operates an invite-only platform for sourcing and contributing multimodal training data, with contributor payouts via Stripe and Wise. As of 2026-07-21 the company publishes no public API, developer documentation, SDK, or machine-readable specifications.
